# 🌸 Wanja AI — Curriculum-Accurate Study Assistant

Wanja AI is a dedicated study and homework equation solver designed with a **Pink, White, and Purple** aesthetic. Unlike standard ChatGPT, Wanja references the **exact textbook name, edition, and author** specified by the student to guarantee curriculum-accurate formulas, methods, and solutions without confusion or conflicting notations.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **🔐 Passcode Gate & Student Identity**:
   - Master Passcode: `BETTYAI` required to enter.
   - Unique usernames: First come, first served.
   - Multi-chat session memory: automatically remembers all past study conversations per user.

2. **📚 Reference Book Directive**:
   - Specify the **Book Title**, **Edition**, and **Author / Writer**.
   - Presets included (Stewart's *Calculus: Early Transcendentals*, Halliday & Resnick's *Fundamentals of Physics*, Campbell's *Biology*, Brown's *Chemistry: The Central Science*, K.A. Stroud's *Engineering Mathematics*, etc.) or enter any custom textbook.
   - All solutions cite and use the exact formula structure and definitions of the selected book.

3. **📷 Direct Phone Camera Scanner & Photo Upload**:
   - Scan questions directly from your phone camera or laptop webcam using the built-in viewfinder.
   - Attach photos, screenshots, or paste images directly (`Ctrl + V`).

4. **📝 Clear Step-by-Step Arrangement**:
   - 🎯 Problem Statement & Given Quantities
   - 📘 Book-specific Formulas & Rules
   - 📝 Step-by-Step Working & Intermediate Calculations
   - 🏁 Boxed Final Answer

5. **💡 Dedicated "Explain" Button**:
   - Every AI solution has a **"💡 Explain"** button.
   - Expands a simple breakdown explaining the big picture, everyday analogies, why each step was taken, and common pitfalls to avoid.

6. **📱 Dual Responsive PC & Smartphone Layout**:
   - Desktop: full collapsible sidebar, chat management, rich mathematical rendering.
   - Smartphone: slide-out drawer, touch-friendly viewfinder scan, bottom quick bar.

7. **⚡ Super-Fast Response**:
   - Instant response time (< 50ms locally) with zero lag.
   - Optional setting to plug in a Gemini API key for open-ended multi-modal live reasoning.
